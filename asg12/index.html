My Favorite Movies

These are my favorite movies because they made me more passionate about animation and film.

Animated Movies

Howl's Moving Castle

Howl's Moving castle is a movie animated by Studio Ghibli founded by the Japanese animator, producer and director, Hayao Miyazaki. It is about how a young lady is transformed into an elder lady and gets warped into a moving castle.

Castle in The Sky

Castle in The Sky is made by the same creators who made Howls Moving Castle. It is about the search of a floating castle and about evading pirates and secret agents. This is one of my favorite movies because I recently watched it with my boyfriend who loves this movie.

How To Train your Dragon

I grew up watching this movie. It's about how a young Viking kid befriends a dragon, which is ironic because his village is against dragons. This forever changes the fate of his village.

Into The SpiderVerse

I watched this when I was a teenager and it was very memorable, because it made me grow my love for animated movies. Made by Sony Animations, it is about how multiple people from different dimensions who are Spider-people team up to save their universe, also known as a multiverse.

Kpop Demon Hunters

I was anticipating the release of this movie as I love K-pop and it's made by the same creators of Into The Spiderverse. It's about K-pop idols who are secretly protectors of their universe who battle demons.

Live Action Movies

Transformers 1 by Michael Bay

I also grew up watching this movie as a kid. It's about Robots who are not from Earth and are aliens who disguise themselves as cars to protect the fate of the Earth.

The Amazing Spiderman with Andrew Garfield

In this Movie, Andrew Garfield plays as Peter Parker who turns into Spider-Man after discovering his powers.

Godzilla: Minus One

I also recently watched this movie with friends, and enjoyed it as it's about how humanity faces Godzilla after the war in Japan.

Comedy Movies

The Interview

In this comedy, a journalist and a producer attempt to interview a dictator.

Rush Hour 2

I love this movie because I love Jackie Chan movies, it's about two detectives who solve crimes in China with action and comedy.

Romance Movies

Life Is Beautiful

In this Italian movie I had watched with my boyfriend, it is about a bittersweet story of love and hope during WWII in Italy.

La La Land

In this musical movie also with romance in it, it's about a romance of a jazz musician and an aspiring actress in L.A!
